The person behind the camera

I'm Augustin, and I'm in my first year of B.Tech in Information Technology. A year ago I could use computers; now I'm slowly learning how they actually think, and it's more fun than I expected.

I like the moment when a messy idea turns into something you can click. I'm not fast yet, and I break things often, but I've learned that a broken page teaches me more than a working one.

Every project I finish makes the next one less scary.

This portfolio is my way of showing where I'm starting. Come back in a year and the story will be longer.
